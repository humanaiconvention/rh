# Working with this repository

- Read README.md first. This is a finite mathematical extract, not an RH proof.
- Preserve literal cutoffs, zero extensions, both bilinear weights, scalar companions, and boundary phases.
- Label a claim as a proved identity, a finite computation, or an open estimate.
- Do not infer cancellation from regrouping, one component, or a finite measurement.
- Run both verification scripts after changing an identity or its arithmetic implementation.
- Keep public notes self-contained. Do not add private research logs, third-party archives, credentials, or unrelated project material.
- A stronger theorem needs a written proof; passing finite tests is insufficient.
