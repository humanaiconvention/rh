The earlier collection carries an MIT notice with **Copyright (c) 2026 humanaiconvention**. Its text is copied unchanged to [the existing project notice](notices/EXISTING-PROJECT-MIT-LICENSE.txt). This preserves the existing notice and repository intent; this assembly makes no new license decision, ownership assertion or completed asset-level rights finding.

Mathlib is a separate dependency at the pinned revision. Its existing Apache-2.0 text is copied unchanged to [the Mathlib notice](notices/MATHLIB-LICENSE.txt). It is not relicensed under the project's MIT notice. Other pinned external dependencies are not vendored here and retain their own terms; this round does not claim a completed audit of each dependency's notice requirements.

The selected proof headers are preserved unchanged. Importing Mathlib theorems does not by itself identify copied proof text, coauthorship or rights to relicense any actual third-party material. If review identifies copied material, the appropriate source/contribution notices must be established before release.

No new blanket root LICENSE has been added as a substitute for that review. A human must confirm the source/contribution rights, applicable notices and author/contact details, and decide the final release-license presentation. Existing notice copies are evidence, not a legal clearance certificate. This is a concrete release blocker, independent of whether the Lean statements compile.
