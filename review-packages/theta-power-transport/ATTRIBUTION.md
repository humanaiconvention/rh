# Attribution and review boundary

The mechanism is the classical centered signed single-crossing comparison. This package instantiates it for the complete, normalized theta and Macdonald densities, with natural-power coordinates and the required actual-source integrability. It does not claim discovery of a new duality.

The proof reuses the generic coordinate comparison and the bundled theta/Macdonald provider chain. Those providers in turn use Mathlib's analysis, measure theory, theta, Gamma and zeta infrastructure. Dependency acknowledgments do not assign coauthorship of the present contribution. The Macdonald integral representation and the standard completed-zeta normalization retain their classical meanings; exact definitions are in [SOURCE-DEFINITIONS.md](SOURCE-DEFINITIONS.md).

Preparation and proof development were AI-assisted. Exact historical model identities and individual human contribution credits have not been authenticated. No model identity, copyright owner, human proof approval or upstream maintainer approval is inferred from saved compilation evidence.

The unchanged sources do not acquire invented contribution headers through this extraction. Repository licensing intent does not establish rights in every contribution or relicense dependencies. Applicable Mathlib notices and Apache 2.0 terms remain distinct from contribution licensing.

Before submission or release, review attribution, notices and rights, current-upstream overlap, API naming and placement, exact density normalization and hypotheses, and any separately selected fresh replay. Saved source acceptance is not a substitute for those gates.
